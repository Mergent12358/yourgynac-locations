Hormone Problems You Can Discuss on a First Endocrinology Consult

Hormone problems are not only about diabetes or thyroid disease. People also come with weight gain they think may be hormone related, irregular cycles that have been linked to PCOS, or a mix of tiredness and reports that do not quite add up. A first consult with an endocrinologist is often about sorting that picture, not about rushing to a conclusion.

Bring whatever reports you already have, even if they feel incomplete. Mention medicines, recent weight change, and, if it applies, pregnancy or plans to conceive, because diabetes or thyroid issues in pregnancy need careful handling. The doctor can tell you which tests are actually useful, and which ones can wait.
