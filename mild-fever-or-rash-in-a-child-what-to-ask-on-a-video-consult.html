Mild Fever or a Rash in a Child: What to Ask on a Video Consult

A mild fever or a new rash is one of the most common reasons parents look up symptoms late in the evening. The internet is full of worst-case lists, and that can make it harder to judge what you are seeing. A short video consult with a pediatrician can help you decide whether this is something to watch at home, or whether the child should be seen in person.

You can speak to child specialist Dr. Deepak Nagar on YourGynac by video, chat or audio. Dr. Deepak Nagar is a pediatrician and neonatologist with more than 8 years of experience. The first consultation on YourGynac is ₹1.

Before the call, write down the temperature, when it started, whether the child is drinking fluids, and if there is a cough, vomiting, or a rash. Take a clear photo of the rash in good light, and keep the child nearby. Ask what warning signs should send you to a hospital the same day.

A video consult is not the right place for a very unwell child, breathing difficulty, or a baby who is unusually sleepy. For milder illnesses, it can still give you a plan and a follow-up point, without rushing straight to a crowded waiting room.
