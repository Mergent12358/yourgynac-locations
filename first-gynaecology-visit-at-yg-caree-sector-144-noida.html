A First Gynaecology Visit at YG Caree in Sector 144, Noida

Sector 144 sits in a part of Noida where many people juggle office hours with family appointments. If you have been meaning to see a gynaecologist, the trip itself can be the thing you keep postponing, especially if you are not sure whether the visit will be a long one.

Dr. Shweta Saini at YG Caree Polyclinic, Sector 144 practises as a gynaecologist and obstetrician at that clinic. She has more than 8 years of experience, and her training includes MBBS, MS, DNB, and an FNB in Maternal and Fetal Medicine. Besides clinic visits, she offers audio, video and chat consults on YourGynac, which can be useful if you first want to talk through symptoms from home.

A first visit is often about periods, PCOS, contraception, pregnancy planning, or a report you want explained. You do not need to have everything figured out before you go. Bring any recent tests, and a short note of your questions. If you start with a video consult, she can tell you whether a clinic examination or scan is the next step.

Your first consultation is ₹1, with a free follow-up included. That makes it easier to ask the first question without waiting for a concern to feel urgent.
